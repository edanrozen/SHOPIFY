## 1. Product Overview

**One line:** A knee support sleeve with a one-handed tension dial, for Israeli
adults whose knee has started deciding what they can and cannot do.

**What it does:** A knit sleeve that wraps the joint in even pressure. A dial
tightens or loosens it with one hand — mid-walk, without taking a shoe off.
Two flexible side stabilisers run the length of the joint, and a perforated gel
ring sits around the kneecap. An internal grip stops it sliding down.

**Category (the shelf buyers search):** מגן ברך / תמיכה לברך — knee brace,
knee support. Not a medical device, and positioned beside pharmacy and
orthopaedic-supply sleeves, not beside sports gear.

**Type:** Single-product e-commerce, dropshipped.

**Pricing & unit economics:**

|---|---|
| Price | 199.99₪ · **עוסק פטור, no VAT is owed** (the store nonetheless charged 27.46₪ on order #1001 — open compliance item, see 12d) |

**Catalogue:** knee brace (hero), massage insoles, bamboo work socks, two

## 2. Target Audience

**Who:** Israeli adults, roughly 45–70, whose knee limits ordinary daily life —
stairs, standing all day, walking, getting out of a low chair. Blue-collar and
on-their-feet workers are over-represented in the creative that performs.

**Jobs to be done:**
1. Get through a full day on my feet without paying for it that evening.
2. Stop planning around my knee — stairs, outings, the grandchildren.
3. Get support **without** a rigid hinged brace or a doctor's appointment.

**Buying trigger:** Not a sudden injury. An accumulation — "it has been like
this for a while and I have stopped doing things."

### ⚠️ Second buyer, and it is not in the targeting

**Several reviewers bought it for someone else.** A daughter for her father, a
wife for her husband:

> "אבא שלי כבר עשור בערך סובל מכאבי ברכיים... הזמנתי" — לימור ל.
> "כבר לא ידעתי מה לעשות עם הברך של בעלי אהרון... החלטתי לקנות לו" — ברכה א.

The ads currently speak only to the sufferer ("גבר, לא נמאס לך"). **An untested
angle exists for the daughter and the wife** — different pain (watching someone
you love stop moving), different language, same product. Treat as a hypothesis
with real evidence behind it, not a proven segment.

## 4. Problems & Pain Points

**The core problem:** The knee has started setting the agenda. Not agony —
*negotiation*. You plan the stairs. You think before getting up. You decline the
walk.

**Verbatim, from reviews:**
- "ההליכות בבוקר הפסיקו להיות קשות סוף סוף אפשר לחזור לעשות הליכה עם הבן"
- "אני לוקחת את הנכדים מהגן נעלמו הכאבים"
- "לפני כמה חודשיים רצינו לעשות טיול קצר ואחריי מאה מטרים הוא לא הצליח לזוז"
- "אני עומד הרבה שעות במהלך היום"
- "הוא פשוט חזר לחיות"

**Why existing solutions fall short:**
- **Velcro-strap sleeves:** set once in the morning, wrong all day. Loosen them
  and you take the shoe off.
- **Hinged braces:** lock the joint. Bulky, visible, feel like an injury.
- **Plain knit sleeves:** no adjustment, no lateral support, slide down.
- **Physio / doctor:** costs time and money, and needs an appointment.

**The emotional tension:** the arithmetic of ageing. Each thing you stop doing
feels permanent. The product's promise is reversal — "חזר לחיות", not "pain
relief".

### The sizing gamble — a pain nobody else addresses

> "סוף סוף אני לא צריך להמר שהמגן ברך יהיה בול עליי ואני יכול פשוט לכוון אותו" — משה פ.

Buying a brace online is a bet on fit. The dial removes the bet. **This is the
single strongest differentiation argument in the whole corpus and it is not
currently the lead message anywhere.**

## 6. Differentiation

1. **The dial, adjustable with one hand mid-movement.** Both the fit guarantee
   and the daily-use advantage.
2. **Two lateral stabilisers** that bound the joint without locking it like a
   hinged brace.
3. **Perforated gel ring** around the kneecap — pressure off the patella, air
   through.
4. **Internal grip** so it does not slide down.
5. **30-day return** where the law requires 14.

**Why someone chooses it:** it is the only option that is simultaneously
adjustable, supportive, and invisible under trousers.

## 10. Brand Voice

Extracted from the ad script the client approved and from the page copy that is
live, not from adjectives.

1. **Plain, physical, concrete.** Named actions, not benefits.
   *"לרדת במדרגות בלי לתכנן את זה מראש."*
2. **Speaks to a man who has been dealing with this a while.** Direct, not
   gentle, never patronising. *"גבר, לא נמאס לך להפסיק לסבול?"*
3. **Specific about the mechanism.** It earns the claim by describing the part.
   *"סיבוב אחד מהדק, סיבוב לאחור משחרר."*
4. **Honest about limits.** States it is not medical treatment, states it is
   sold as one unit.
5. **No fake urgency.** 10,000 units in stock; "only 3 left" would be a lie, and
   on a brand-new store a small lie is the thing that kills it.

**Tone shifts:** ads are blunt and conversational; the page is calm and
explanatory; email is short and low-pressure ("הסל שמור לך, אפשר להשלים מתי
שנוח"); anything legal or medical is flat and factual.

## 12c. Tax status: עוסק פטור — binding on every financial calculation

**Edan trades as an עוסק פטור (exempt dealer), confirmed by him 24.11.2026.
He does not charge VAT and does not remit it.**

### What this changes

**199.99₪ is the full revenue per unit.** Nothing is deducted for VAT. Every
earlier calculation that treated the price as VAT-inclusive and netted it down
was wrong, and the figures below replace them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Revenue per unit | **199.99₪** |
| Landed cost | **~80₪** (split between product / shipping / duty is `MISSING`) |
| Gross profit per unit | **~120₪** |
| Payment processing | **`MISSING` — not supplied.** At a typical ~3% + ~1₪ it is ~7₪ |
| **Break-even CPA** | **~113₪** (120₪ gross less ~7₪ fees) — supersedes the 107₪ recorded earlier |
| Break-even ROAS | **~1.67** |

An exempt dealer also **cannot reclaim VAT on inputs**. Any Israeli import VAT
inside the 80₪ is a sunk cost, not recoverable — which is one of the arguments
for registering as a מורשה once volume justifies it.

### Black Friday is live, and it moves the break-even

From 24.11, Kaching Bundles runs a Black Friday offer on the product page.
**Verified by rendering the live page in a mobile browser** — the variant
price fields in the Admin API do **not** reflect app-level bundle discounts,
so a GraphQL check reported full price and was wrong.

What a buyer actually sees:

| Offer | Price | Was |
|---|---|---|
| Single unit | **180.00₪** | 199.99₪ struck through, labelled 10% Black Friday |
| Two units + free socks | **259.99₪** | 399.98₪, "saving 139.99₪" |

**Single-unit economics at the offer price:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Revenue | **180₪** |
| Landed cost | ~80₪ |
| Gross profit | **~100₪** |
| **Break-even CPA** | **~93₪** after payment fees — down from ~113₪ |
| Break-even ROAS | **~1.80** |

**Every media plan while the offer runs uses 93₪, not 113₪.** The two-unit
bundle has better unit economics and is the thing worth steering buyers to,
which no creative currently does.

**Lesson recorded:** app-level discounts are invisible to the Admin API's
price fields. To know what a customer sees, render the page.

### 🔴 The turnover ceiling is a growth constraint, not a detail

The 2026 exempt-dealer ceiling is reported as **122,833₪ of annual turnover**
(2025: 120,000₪). **Turnover, not profit.**
[Source](https://www.bizportal.co.il/additives/312/vat-dealer-status-ca.html),
read 24.11.2026 — **not from the Tax Authority directly; treat as UNVERIFIED
until gov.il is checked.**

At 199.99₪ per unit that ceiling is:

- **~614 units per year**
- **~51 units per month**
- **~1.7 units per day**

A campaign that works lands him over the ceiling inside a year. Crossing it
forces registration as an **עוסק מורשה** mid-year, which means charging 18% VAT
on top of — or out of — the same 199.99₪ price. If the price stays, revenue per
unit drops to ~169.5₪ and **break-even CPA falls from ~113₪ to ~82₪**: the media
plan changes overnight.

**This is not a reason to slow down.** It is a reason to decide, before the
ceiling arrives, whether the price rises to absorb VAT or the margin absorbs it.
Every agent modelling growth past ~50 units a month must state which assumption
it used.

### Still missing before any number here is trustworthy

Payment processing fee · Shopify monthly plan cost · the 80₪ split ·
actual return rate (no orders yet) · the ceiling verified at gov.il.

**1.1 — 2026-11-24.** Added three avatars (§3) from the `customer-research`
skill. Added two research files: `variants/dofek/research/market-survey-2026-11-24.md`
(40+ listings on 7 platforms) and `competitor-tredvik-2026-11-24.md`.

Two corrections to 1.0: the dial is **not** unique — Amazon carries a crowded
category of dial braces including SOZO with a licensed BOA dial, so the moat is
geographic and temporary, not a product advantage. And comparable braces ship
from Shenzhen at 47–60₪ against an 80₪ landed cost, making sourcing the highest
-return lever on the table.

**Tredvik (tredvik.com) is a direct competitor selling the same dial brace**
at $39.99 **buy-one-get-one** with a 99-day guarantee, across its own store,
Amazon and Walmart. **The customer pays ~148₪ and receives two sleeves**, against
199.99₪ for one at DOFEK — 35% cheaper for twice the quantity. The first unit is
not discounted; the second is given, so it is a perceived-value offer rather than
a price cut, which is what keeps their unit margin intact. Three
independent sources report unanswered support, knockoff product, hidden
ownership and return shipping charged to the customer. **Trust, not product, is
where DOFEK can be positioned.**

**1.2 — 2026-11-24.** Reframed Tredvik: it sells in the US and does not
advertise in Israel, so it is **not a competitor** — it is a proxy audience
source, with hundreds of reviews where DOFEK has 13 imported ones. Mined in
`variants/dofek/research/competitor-tredvik-2026-11-24.md`.

Five things that change the brief:

* **Shipping is not the blocker.** A buyer waited a month and wrote "it was
  worth the wait". DOFEK's 10-14 business days is half that. Stop hedging it on
  the page; state it as a certainty instead.
* **The language is stability, not pain relief** — "more stability walking and
  climbing stairs", "stay steady on my feet all afternoon", "does not feel like
  a rigid orthopedic brace". Safer legally and closer to how they speak.
* **Sizing fails a third time**, in a third independent source. Tredvik also
  sells left- and right-specific sleeves and ships the wrong one. **Check
  whether the DOFEK brace is universal** — if it is, "fits either leg, you
  cannot order the wrong one" is an unused advantage.
* **Re-tightening is expected, not a flaw:** "I did have to adjust the sleeve a
  little, but I think that's normal for any sleeve covering a moving joint."
  For every other brand that is a tolerated weakness. For DOFEK it is the dial.
* **Stairs and prolonged standing** are the two activities that recur in both
  markets.

Adjacent segment seen once, not built on: post knee-replacement patients.

**1.3 — 2026-11-24.** Added a fourth avatar aimed at older buyers, 65-80:
`variants/dofek/research/avatar-seniors-2026-11-24.md`. Confidence medium, five
local data points plus two foreign; the channel and payment implications are
flagged as assumptions from Israeli market context, not findings.

What separates it from avatar 1: there the knee trims the routine, here it
threatens independence itself, and the unspoken fear is becoming dependent. The
trigger is always in front of someone else — a grandchild, a son, a family trip.

Three implications that change execution:

* **The current creative may actively repel this group.** A drawn "לחץ כאן"
  button with a cursor hand reads as an advert rather than a shop, and "גבר, לא
  נמאס לך" lands as shouting at an older man. Untested — worth an A/B before
  concluding.
* **The purchase is a conversation**, with a spouse, an adult child or a friend
  ("חברה טובה סיפרה לי"). Creative has to survive being forwarded on WhatsApp
  and still explain itself.
* **Bit and instalments are defaults at this age in Israel, and neither exists
  at checkout.** This is the best-fitting hypothesis for seven of eight checkout
  sessions leaving before typing an email. It is a hypothesis, not a finding,
  and the client has said payment works.

Also: no phone number on the page. At this age a phone number is a trust signal
whether or not anyone calls it.
